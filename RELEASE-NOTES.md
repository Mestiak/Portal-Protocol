# Portal Protocol 0.8.7 — Patch Notes

## 🧭 Raids & Strikes — Part 2 of 3

This release is **part 2 of 3** in the raids and strikes mechanics pass. Part 1
brought Vale Guardian teleports, Deimos oils and Dhuum CM pick ups to the Stats
modal. This part covers Statues of Darkness and Ura, and makes Deimos oils count
every slick. More encounters are coming in part 3.

---

## ⚔️ Encounter Tabs

**Statues of Darkness — Stun Overrides** — see every Daze, Fear or Knockdown applied to either eye: who did it, which skill did it, and how long the eye stayed incapacitated. The light orb's own Flare stuns appear alongside them, so you can tell a planned stun from an accidental override.

**Filter by Eye** — switch between Both eyes, Eye of Judgment only and Eye of Fate only; rankings, counts and the feed all follow, which makes it easy to spot someone using the wrong control on the wrong side.

**Eye Teleports** — when an eye blinks to a new spot, that moment is marked on the same timeline in orange. A missed stun now has an explanation instead of a mystery gap.

**Skill Names You Can Trust** — skills are named from a generated table of crowd-control skills built from official GW2 data, so far more rows name the real skill — including mech and minion commands such as Jade Mortar and Artillery Slash that used to read as unknown.

**Deimos — Oils, Reworked** — oils are counted from each slick's own damage instead of its first-hit trigger, so no pool goes missing, and every down is credited to the slick that actually dealt the damage.

**Ura — Bloodstone Shards** — a Shards tab for every mode of Ura: every pick-up, which geyser lost its Hardened Crust, who was freed from the Pressure Blast bubbles and how long each person floated. Shards lost when the carrier went down are reported too.

**Shard Mistakes, Explained** — when a shard did nothing, the tab says why — out of range, early, wasted, or beaten by another player who cleared that wave or geyser first. Those labels are inferred from the log, so the combat replay is still the final word.
